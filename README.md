# Arnova Group Agency

Arnova Group Agency helps businesses in Rwanda and the rest of Africa with websites, simple mobile apps, Google Maps (Google Business Profile) setup, and search visibility.

The agency is not registered yet and is taking first clients. This repository does not present a client portfolio.

Created by Arnold Rurangwa / Arnova Group.

## Services

- **Business websites** — mobile-ready sites with listed package prices in Rwandan francs. Domains at cost; first-year .co.rw is included in website packages. A launch offer waives the first-year .co.rw domain (normally 9,500 RWF) for the first 5 website clients. Package prices stay as listed.
- **Mobile apps** — a simple first app or prototype from 72,000 RWF, with no domain included. This is not a store-quality or large-scale app. Larger apps are quoted.
- **Google Maps setup** — Google Business Profile setup, quoted after a free check.
- **Search visibility** — practical work to help a business be found more clearly on Google Search, quoted after a free check.

Arnova is not affiliated with Google, and we do not guarantee search rankings or Maps placement.

An online shop with real MoMo or card checkout is 145,000 RWF and includes first-year paid hosting, because a checkout shop cannot use free static hosting.

## Client website design policy

Every business website must have its **own visual identity**, informed by its logo, signage, approved photographs, audience and industry. Arnova Group Agency's own brand colors and page template must **not** be reused automatically for clients. Start with a client-specific creative brief; vary color palette, layout, typography and content. Use business images only with proper permission and do not present unapproved concepts as official sites. See [Client Website Design Standards](docs/CLIENT_WEBSITE_DESIGN_STANDARDS.md).

## Delivery and client approval

Public releases, domain changes, Google Business Profile edits, invoices, sales outreach and live payment activation require the business owner's permission. Uncommissioned concepts must be labeled clearly, avoid copying unauthorized photographs or logos, and must not imply affiliation with those businesses or with Google.

**Approval gate:** the team researches requirements and content, implements the work, reviews it with the client, and launches after approval. The client owns their domain registration and the content they provide.

See [client design standards](docs/CLIENT_WEBSITE_DESIGN_STANDARDS.md), [cross-platform guidance](docs/CROSS_PLATFORM_WEBSITE_SUPPORT.md) and [public repo compatibility audit](docs/GITHUB_PLATFORM_AUDIT.md).

## Concept work

Concept sites on the public website are **uncommissioned design proposals**, not verified clients or authorized business websites. Before onboarding any business, verify the map listing, establish owner contact and permission, confirm copy and real images, agree on scope and a price, and only then publish a business-owned production site.
