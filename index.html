Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can you build a website for a business in Rwanda or elsewhere in Africa?

Yes. We work with businesses in Rwanda and the rest of Africa. We can discuss a clear, mobile-friendly website sized to your business needs and budget.

Can customers contact my business through WhatsApp?

We can design a WhatsApp enquiry flow where it suits your business. Messaging requires a working number and customer consent.

Do you provide online payments or booking systems?

These can be scoped where suitable, but payment and booking integrations depend on the providers, requirements and approvals involved.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can review its structure, visual design, mobile experience and maintainability before proposing changes.

Do you build mobile apps?

Yes. We can build a simple first app or prototype, from 72,000 RWF. A domain is not included. This is not a store-quality or large-scale app. Larger apps are quoted after we review the scope.

Are you part of Google, and do you guarantee rankings?

No. Arnova is not affiliated with Google. We can help set up a Google Business Profile and work on search visibility, but we do not guarantee search rankings or Maps placement.

How do we get started?

Send a short description of your business and whether you need a website, a simple mobile app prototype, Google Maps setup, or help with search visibility.

Can I pay in another currency?

You can request a quote in another currency. We confirm the exchange rate, the exact invoice amount and the available receiving channel before payment. Published prices are in Rwandan francs. We accept MTN MoMo Pay and bank transfer.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Our standard website packages are one-time builds. Monthly care and other maintenance are optional. Domain and hosting renewal costs apply when the first-year period ends.

Can I pay in two parts?

You can request an approved staged payment plan. We will write the amounts, due dates and delivery conditions in the quotation before starting work.

What if I need more pages or online payments?

We provide a custom quote for added pages, booking systems, databases, online checkout, integrations and other features outside the selected package.

Who owns my website?

Ownership, domain registration, access credentials, third-party subscriptions and any transfer arrangements are defined in your written agreement before payment.

What should be on a first website?

For many local businesses, a clear one-page site is a good start. It can list your real services, a working phone number or WhatsApp link, your correct location, photos you have permission to use, and one clear next step — contact, book, enquire or visit.
